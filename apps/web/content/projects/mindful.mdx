## Overview

Mindful is a wellness app that helps users build sustainable daily habits through gentle reminders, progress visualization, and guided routines. The challenge was creating an experience that motivates without overwhelming.

## The Problem

Existing wellness apps suffered from two extremes: either too simplistic (basic habit checkboxes) or too complex (overwhelming dashboards and metrics). Users wanted something that felt supportive, not demanding.

## Research

I conducted 15 user interviews and analyzed usage patterns from three competing apps. Key findings:

- Users abandoned apps that required more than 30 seconds of daily input
- Visual progress (streaks, charts) was the top motivator for retention
- Push notification fatigue was the #1 reason for uninstalls

## Design Approach

The interface centers on a single daily view — what's ahead today, what's done, and a gentle nudge for what's next. No dashboards, no deep menus on the home screen.

### Key decisions

- **One-tap logging** — mark a habit complete in a single tap from the home screen
- **Adaptive reminders** — notification timing adjusts based on when users actually complete habits
- **Progress rings** — circular visualizations that fill over the week, more motivating than streaks that break

## Results

After a 6-week beta with 200 users:
- 72% daily active retention (vs. 35% industry average)
- Average session time: 22 seconds
- NPS score: 68
